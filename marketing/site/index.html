Cash to ash?

Slow down, research, decide, learn.

Read the docs Source on GitHub

Weekly research for a small stock portfolio, with the trade left in your hands.

One week, on one sheet.

CashAsh runs on your own machine. Once a week it researches each holding against the thesis you wrote, proposes a few constrained actions, and sends them to your phone. There is nothing to press. You trade at your broker, or you don't. Sync the statement and the ledger says which advice you followed.

The run, in order.

  1. 1Sync

    Prices, FX, earnings dates, consensus. A failed fetch keeps last week's prices, marked stale. By default the run fires on {{WEEKLY_RUN_DAY}} at {{WEEKLY_RUN_TIME}}.

  2. 2Snapshot

    What the portfolio is worth, pinned as reported that day. A price corrected later cannot rewrite it. An unpriced holding is left out and counted as missing, never as zero.

  3. 3Triage

    One call over the whole portfolio, ranking holdings by what changed in each thesis. The ones passed over are recorded too.

  4. 4Research

    Questions planned from your thesis, answered from evidence. A thesis that has actually changed gets a proposed revision beside it, never a rewrite.

  5. 5Decide

    BUY, ADD, HOLD, TRIM, EXIT, REVIEW, or keep cash. Then the rules run, in code, on whatever was proposed. Every refusal is kept.

  6. 6Report

    One message. Whether anything needs you, a picture of the portfolio computed without a model, the run stage by stage with its time and cost, one line per decision. Failures and refusals sit in a collapsed section, never dropped.

  7. 7You

    The next morning. Read it. Trade or don't, at your broker. Sync the statement, and the ledger says which advice you followed.

The rules are code, not a request.

A prompt asking a model to respect a limit is a suggestion. The limits are applied afterwards, in code, to whatever it proposed. These are the shipped defaults. Each one is a setting.

  • No position above {{MAX_POSITION_WEIGHT_PCT}}% of the portfolio.The batch is checked as a whole, so five small buys cannot do what one large one may not.
  • One trade up to €{{MAX_NEW_TRADE_EUR}}. One week up to €{{MAX_WEEKLY_ALLOCATION_EUR}}.The week's allowance is spent across reruns, so running twice does not double it.
  • Only cash that is actually there funds a buy.Planned contributions are noted, never spent.
  • A buy passes four checks or none: reason, price, fit, size.A thesis rated moderate or better. A valuation checked against the company's own history. The position under the cap and each theme it carries under {{CONCENTRATION_ALERT_PCT}}%. At least €{{MIN_TRADE_EUR}} once every limit applies. Nothing supplies valuation evidence yet, so today every buy is refused, by name. That is deliberate.
  • A sale needs one of three reasons, and a bad week is not one.The thesis you wrote has broken, and a broken thesis may exit where a weakened one may only trim. You examined the holding and recorded that there is no reason to own it. Or it is over the cap by more than new money could dilute, and only the excess goes.
  • A price older than {{PRICE_STALE_AFTER_DAYS}} days, or a missing FX rate, blocks any trade.A review still goes through. Money does not.
  • Advice expires after {{RECOMMENDATION_EXPIRY_DAYS}} days.The next run replaces it. Nothing can be recorded against advice a later run withdrew.

Every default lives in src/config.py beside a note on how it was chosen. main.py process prints the ones in force.

Every finding carries a label.

  • sourcedCites a supplied evidence item by ID and quotes it exactly. URL and date come from the item, not the model. An unknown ID or an invented quote demotes it to unanswered.
  • backgroundWhat the model brought with it. Never enough on its own to move money.
  • unansweredStays unanswered, in storage and on your phone. An open question is a finding.

A quote check proves the excerpt exists, not that the conclusion follows. That part is still reading.

Your thesis is yours. Research that finds it weakened writes a proposed version beside it and stops. The baseline the system is judged against stays the one you wrote.

It will probably cost more than it earns.

The portfolio this was built for is small enough that a weekly pipeline over several models can cost more in a year than any excess return it could earn. That is the accepted trade: a better research habit and an interesting system, not a return.

So models route cheap by default, and the expensive ones are opt-in and metered. Nothing a model says carries investment authority. Its stated confidence is not a probability and is never stored as one.

The evals measure the process, because that is what can be measured here:

  • the output has the shape it was asked for;
  • every claim that says it has a source actually has one;
  • the rules were obeyed, and each refusal was recorded;
  • no figure was invented;
  • a rerun gives the same answer.

Whether the advice was good is not measurable at this scale. Outcomes are recorded beside the same money left in a {{BENCHMARK_NAME}} tracker. Recorded, never claimed.

The record answers one more question: what following the advice would be worth now. Trades you made and trades you skipped are totalled separately, every total carries its trade count, and it starts at the first recommendation and only grows forward. A model asked what it would have said last year already knows what happened next.

Start from a screenshot of your broker.

Type in what your broker shows: ticker, quantity, value, blended return. Cost basis is derived from those and the total is reconciled against the broker's figure, so a typo cannot become your cost basis. The file stays in your home directory, never in the repository.

# one profile, one snapshot, one database
uv run python main.py profile add adam --telegram-id 123456789 --operator
cp seed_snapshot.example.toml ~/Documents/cash-ash/profiles/adam/seed_snapshot.toml
uv run python main.py init --dry-run   # does it reconcile?
uv run python main.py init

# then, each week
uv run python main.py weekly --telegram

# after you trade, from your phone: /refresh pulls the statement and imports the fills

Every command, the weekly cycle and the evidence format are in the docs. What is not built yet is on the roadmap.
