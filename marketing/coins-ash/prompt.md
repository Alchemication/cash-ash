# Generation brief

The brief given to ChatGPT's image model for `source.png`. The first prompt is
the one that produced the shipped image; the variant was not used.

## Prompt

A single 1200x630 landscape image, dark and quiet. A stack of a few euro coins
sits on a near-black charcoal surface, lit from one side by a warm ember glow.
The bottom coin is crumbling at its edge into fine grey ash, and a handful of
tiny orange sparks lift off it and drift upward to the right, fading as they
rise. Nothing else in frame. Palette: warm near-black (#141416) background,
bone off-white (#ece6da) coin highlights, one hot orange (#ff6a1f) for the
sparks and the glow. Matte, grainy film texture, shallow depth of field,
slight vignette. No text, no logos, no hands, no charts, no currency symbols
other than the coins themselves. Mood: calm, slightly ominous, not dramatic.
The sparks should be sparse — twenty at most — and small.

## Unused variant: hero backdrop

Same scene, 16:9, with the subject pushed to the lower right third and the
upper left two-thirds left as nearly empty dark surface with faint drifting
ash, so headline text can sit over it. Even quieter than the first: the coin
stack is a detail, not the subject.
